# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
Open Circuit community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, sexualized
language or attention, publishing private information without permission, and
other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the project maintainers through the repository's
private security/contact channel. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and
addressed fairly.
